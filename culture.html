History & Language Roots

Breton is a Brythonic Celtic language with deep maritime and oral traditions. It arrived in Armorica (modern Brittany) in the early medieval period and developed distinct regional dialects.

Major Cultural Aspects

  • Breton music: festoù-noz, gwerz, and kan ha diskan singing styles
  • Traditional dance and communal gatherings
  • Crafts, stories, and place-based oral histories

Mythology & Folklore

Legends of sea-spirits, magical wells, and saints combine with everyday life; these stories are powerful learning tools. We run story nights and recording sessions to keep them alive.
