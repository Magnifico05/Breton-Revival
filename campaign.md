# Breton Revival Campaign — Strategy Brief

## Goal

- Increase awareness and daily use of Breton among local communities and diaspora; grow measurable participation in classes, events, and mentoring programs within 12 months.

## Objectives & KPIs

- Increase website visits by 200% in 6 months.
- Reach 50k impressions across social channels in first 3 months.
- Recruit 200 new master–apprentice signups and 50 volunteer mentors within 12 months.
- Fill 60% capacity for the first festival and immersion trips.

## Target Audiences

- Local families (parents of young children)
- Young adults (16–35) interested in music and culture
- Language learners and heritage speakers in the diaspora
- Cultural institutions and local government partners

## Core Channels & Tactics

- Website: central hub with events, signups, resources, and a blog.
- Social media (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok): daily Word-of-the-Day, short music clips, learner testimonials.
- Local radio & community posters: festival & immersion promotion.
- Partnerships: schools, cultural centers, local artists.
- Email newsletter: monthly highlights and volunteer opportunities.

## Campaign Elements (media to produce)

- Website landing pages for events, programs, and resources (done).
- Daily Word-of-the-Day short videos and static posts (5–10 templates).
- 60–90s promotional video with music highlights and community voices.
- Printable posters for community boards and schools.
- Social-first short clips of learners, mentors, and musical highlights.

## Content Themes

- "Why it matters" — short stories from speakers and youth
- Music & movement — festivals and workshops
- Family & early learning — language nests and practical tips
- Myth & place — folklore to anchor learning

## Timeline (first 6 months)

1. Month 0–1: Build landing pages, create initial post templates, recruit partners
2. Month 2–3: Launch Word-of-the-Day social push, schedule festival lineup
3. Month 4: Run Immersion Week pilot; collect testimonials
4. Month 5–6: Festival execution; measure engagement; iterate

## Creative Notes

- Use bold imagery, strong typography (Cinzel for headlines), and a warm Celtic green + gold palette.
- Keep posts short, voice friendly and encouraging.

## Production Checklist

- Social templates (square and story sizes)
- Video shoot schedule and voice talent
- Print-ready posters and handouts
- Tracking: UTM links, signup forms, and simple CRM to capture leads

## Next Steps

- Produce 10 social assets (static and short video)
- Book festival performers and venues
- Create volunteer onboarding materials and training for mentors
