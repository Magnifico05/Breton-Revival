[60-90s Promotional Video Script — "Breton Revival"]

Opening (0-10s)
- Visuals: Coastal sunrise, close-up of an old map, children running along a cliff path.
- Voice-over: "This is a language. This is a story. This is home."

Introduce the problem (10-25s)
- Visuals: Empty classroom, archival photos, elder speaking to camera.
- Voice-over: "Breton has been spoken here for centuries. Today, fewer young people hear it at home."
- On-screen text: "Endangered, but not forgotten."

What we do (25-50s)
- Visuals: Mentor and learner cooking together, music festival clips, language nest scenes.
- Voice-over: "We bring people together — mentors and learners, festivals and trips, songs and stories. Everyday use brings the language back."
- B-roll: Word-of-the-Day social clips, volunteers prepping posters.

Call to action (50-75s)
- Visuals: Smiling faces, group singing, event flyers being handed out.
- Voice-over: "Join the revival. Learn one word, sing one song, host one hour. Sign up to volunteer, attend a festival, or become a mentor."
- On-screen CTA: "bretonrevival.example — Sign up today"

End (75-90s)
- Visuals: Sunset over harbor, logo lockup, social handles.
- Voice-over: "Keep Breton living. Be part of the story."
- Music: Short, evocative Breton tune.

Notes for production
- Aim for authentic, community-led footage.
- Keep captions for social versions; add subtitles in English and Breton.
